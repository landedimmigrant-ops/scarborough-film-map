SCARBOROUGH

A landscape film about the whole of a place.

The film

Scarborough gets talked about more than it gets seen. This film gives it the frame: the bluffs and the creeks, the towers and the strip plazas, the temples and churches that share parking lots, the farmland it was and the city it became.

Scarborough occupies a rare historical and cultural space in the world — a suburb that grew into one of the most multicultural places anywhere, and did it quietly, plaza by plaza. The film tells the tale of the city through interviews with the people who live it, moving across its landscapes and its points of view: the shoreline and the high-rise, the kitchen and the congregation, the long memory and the new arrival.

It is being made from the ground, one location at a time.

Put your Scarborough on the map

The locations are the spine of this film — corners, businesses, parks and buildings that actually mean something here. If you know a place that belongs in it, put it on the map. Two minutes, and you can be credited in the film if we shoot there.
